Frequently asked questions

Answers to your questions

Here you'll find answers to the most frequently asked questions about our 3D services.

What 3D printing services do you offer?

We offer FDM 3D printing with Bambu Lab P1S, X1C, and A1 Mini printers. Our printer fleet (9+ machines) ensures high precision and speed. We work with PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, Nylon, PC, and Carbon Fiber materials. The bottom line: printing prices start at 3€ per hour (min. 10€), and we can handle both small individual orders and larger batches.

How does the 3D modeling process work?

We turn your ideas into production-ready 3D models. The process starts with a free consultation to understand your needs. We then perform technical modeling in Fusion 360 or OnShape, prepare STL/STEP files, and test the prototype. Modeling costs 30€ per hour. You should know: the average project takes 2-8 hours depending on complexity.

What is the turnaround time?

Standard project turnaround is 1-3 business days. For larger or more complex projects, deadlines are agreed upon individually. We respond to inquiries within 24 hours. For urgent orders, we offer an express option — contact us to arrange the timeline.

How much does 3D printing cost?

3D printing costs 3€ per hour (minimum 10€, excluding VAT). 3D modeling — 30€ per hour (excluding VAT). For prototyping projects, pricing is calculated individually after consultation. We maintain a transparent pricing policy — you'll know the exact costs before work begins. All prices are excluding VAT.

How do I submit a 3D printing project?

Step by step:

  1. Fill out the submission form, specifying material type, color, and project description.
  2. If you have an STL/STEP file, include a link to it (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).
  3. We'll reply within 24 hours with a price estimate and next steps.
  4. Once agreed, we start work and keep you updated on progress.

You can also call us at +371 24 424 434 or email razosana@bratus.lv.

Why choose Bambu Lab printers?

Bambu Lab is a world-class 3D printer manufacturer known for outstanding print quality and speed. Our experience shows: Bambu Lab printers deliver up to 40% faster printing compared to previous-generation printers, while maintaining high precision. Our fleet includes 9+ machines — from A1 Mini to the professional X1C, capable of printing up to 16 colors in a single project. Source: Bambu Lab official documentation and our practical experience with 500+ completed projects.
